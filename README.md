## 🚀 Task Overview

### [Task 1: Iris Flower Classification](file:///c:/Users/PRACHIGARG/OneDrive/Documents/OASIS%20INFOBYTE%20INTERNSHIP/Task-1)
- **Goal**: Classify Iris flowers into three species (Setosa, Versicolor, Virginica) based on sepal and petal measurements.
- **Model**: Decision Tree / Random Forest Classifier.
- **Outcome**: Achieved high accuracy in distinguishing species using feature-based classification.

### [Task 2: Unemployment Analysis with Python](file:///c:/Users/PRACHIGARG/OneDrive/Documents/OASIS%20INFOBYTE%20INTERNSHIP/Task-2)
- **Goal**: Analyze the unemployment rate in India, especially focusing on the impact of the COVID-19 pandemic.
- **Key Analysis**: Visualization of unemployment rates across different states and regions using interactive plots.
- **Outcome**: Identified significant spikes in unemployment during the lockdown periods.

### [Task 3: Car Price Prediction with Machine Learning](file:///c:/Users/PRACHIGARG/OneDrive/Documents/OASIS%20INFOBYTE%20INTERNSHIP/Task-3)
- **Goal**: Predict the selling price of used cars based on factors like brand, year, mileage, and fuel type.
- **Model**: Random Forest Regressor.
- **Outcome**: Developed a model with an **R-squared score of 0.96**, providing highly accurate price estimations.

### [Task 4: Email Spam Detection with Machine Learning](file:///c:/Users/PRACHIGARG/OneDrive/Documents/OASIS%20INFOBYTE%20INTERNSHIP/Task-4)
- **Goal**: Build a classifier to distinguish between spam and ham (non-spam) messages.
- **Model**: Multinomial Naive Bayes with TF-IDF Vectorization.
- **Outcome**: Achieved **97% accuracy** in detecting spam messages.

### [Task 5: Sales Prediction Using Python](file:///c:/Users/PRACHIGARG/OneDrive/Documents/OASIS%20INFOBYTE%20INTERNSHIP/Task-5)
- **Goal**: Predict product sales based on advertising expenditures on TV, Radio, and Newspaper.
- **Model**: Linear Regression.
- **Outcome**: Identified TV as the primary driver of sales, with the model explaining **90% of the variance** in sales data.
